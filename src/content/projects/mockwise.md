MockWise is an AI-driven mock interview simulator built to help users
practice and sharpen their communication skills ahead of real interviews.
Rather than just recording answers, it focuses on **grammar-level feedback** —
catching phrasing, tense, and structural issues that are easy to miss when
you're focused on the content of your answer.

## The problem

Most interview prep tools focus on *what* you say — the STAR method, keyword
coverage, that kind of thing. Very few look closely at *how* you say it.
For a lot of candidates, especially non-native English speakers, small
grammar slips are what quietly undercut an otherwise strong answer. MockWise
targets that gap specifically.

## How it works

- Users go through a simulated interview flow with role- and industry-specific
  question sets.
- Responses are analyzed for grammatical accuracy, sentence structure, and
  clarity — not just sentiment or keyword matching.
- Feedback is delivered per-answer, so users can see exactly where a response
  lost precision and revise before moving to the next question.

## Status
